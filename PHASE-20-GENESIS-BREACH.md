# Phase 20 — Genesis Breach Vertical Slice

## Player-facing improvements
- Three-wave combat pacing instead of six simultaneous enemies.
- Dedicated Grid Warden mini-boss with 220 integrity and a persistent boss health bar.
- Tactical recovery between waves: energy refill and emergency repair nodes when integrity is low.
- Campaign mission timer and S/A/B/C completion rank.
- Stage-specific environmental guidance: relay path, combat pylons, and extraction-cycle beacon.
- Checkpoint persistence for wave progression and campaign time.

## Encounter structure
1. Relay infiltration and contextual onboarding.
2. Wave 1: two Strikers teach attack rhythm and dodging.
3. Wave 2: Archer, Bulwark, and Hunter create mixed pressure.
4. Wave 3: Grid Warden mini-boss.
5. Four-gate Flux Cycle extraction.
