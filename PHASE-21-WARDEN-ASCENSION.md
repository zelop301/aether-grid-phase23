# Phase 21 — Warden Ascension

Phase 21 improves combat quality instead of adding another separate game mode.

## Grid Warden phase structure

1. **Vector Barrage** — ranged pressure with longer readable telegraphs.
2. **Close Assault** — faster pursuit, short lunges, and tighter attack recovery.
3. **Terminal Overdrive** — maximum aggression with recurring unblockable attacks that must be dodged.

Phase changes occur at approximately 66% and 33% integrity. Each transition briefly staggers the boss, restores a small amount of player Combat Energy and Resolve, creates a distinct impact burst, updates the HUD, and writes the checkpoint.

## Combat readability

- Cyan signals identify attacks suited for parrying.
- Yellow signals identify attacks that can be guarded or parried.
- Red signals identify unblockable attacks that require a dodge.
- Enemy warnings appear near the attacker rather than relying only on large floor rings.

## Combat feel

- Added layered impact rings, slash flashes, particles, and boss-transition bursts.
- Added procedural sounds for light hits, rapid hits, heavy impacts, parries, dodges, boss phases, and boss defeat.
- Added boss-aware lock-on camera framing.
- Added controlled impact camera impulses that respect Reduced Motion and Camera Impact settings.

## Mastery reporting

The campaign tracks:

- Perfect parries
- Phase dodges
- Guard impacts
- Maximum combo chain
- Total damage taken

These statistics appear in the mission report and contribute to the final mission rank.
