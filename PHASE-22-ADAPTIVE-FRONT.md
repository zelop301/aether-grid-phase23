# Phase 22 — Adaptive Front

Phase 22 improves combat intelligence and tactical choice without adding more attack buttons.

## Adaptive squad intelligence

- Added a runtime Tactical Director.
- Enemy formations switch between Probe, Pressure, Surround, Suppress, Hunt, Warden, Assault, and Overdrive tactics.
- Tactics respond to enemy composition, player integrity, campaign wave, difficulty, and Grid Warden phase.
- Attack channels now scale from one to three depending on difficulty and encounter pressure.
- Enemy movement uses role-aware spacing, orbit direction, suppression distance, flanking, and aggression modifiers.

## Aether Conduits

- Added four interactive arena conduits.
- Approach a charged conduit and press E, B on gamepad, or LINK on touch controls.
- Overloads damage and stagger nearby Wardens.
- Successful overloads restore Combat Energy and Resolve.
- Each conduit has a 12-second cooldown and visible recharge state.
- Conduit use contributes to score and the Genesis Breach mission report.

## User experience

- Added live squad tactic, threat, attack-channel, and conduit status to the HUD.
- Added contextual onboarding for conduit use during the tactical formation wave.
- The existing E/B context control now prioritizes nearby conduits, then attempts a finisher.
- Reduced-control users can use the same contextual action instead of learning another combat key.

## Compatibility

Phase 22 preserves the three-wave campaign, Grid Warden phases, checkpoints, combat mastery, Flux Cycle escape, driving assistance, rider rig, and continuous autopilot loop.
