# Phase 23 — Legacy Ascent

Phase 23 turns Genesis Breach into a replayable progression loop without increasing control complexity.

## Persistent operator profile

The browser now stores a sanitized local profile containing:

- Operator level and mastery XP
- Core Fragment total
- Completed Genesis Breach runs
- Best rank, score, and mission time
- Discovered mission contracts
- Selected operator doctrine

Progression is local-only and does not require an account or network connection.

## Operator doctrines

- **Balanced Core — Level 1:** Standard behavior.
- **Vanguard — Level 2:** Starts with 20 Resolve and deals 8% more combat damage.
- **Sentinel — Level 3:** Adds 10 maximum integrity and 45 ms to the parry window.
- **Velocity — Level 4:** Reduces collision damage by 25% and reduces boost drain by 20%.

## Risk-reward modifiers

- **Standard Link:** Normal campaign balance and rewards.
- **Hostile Overdrive:** One additional attack channel, 20% more incoming damage, and 1.25x progression rewards.
- **Fragile Link:** Maximum integrity is limited to 70%, with 1.35x rewards.
- **Null Repair:** Repair nodes are disabled, with 1.20x rewards.

## Repeatable mission contracts

- Perfect Defense — 3 perfect parries
- Conduit Control — 2 conduit overloads
- Clean Extraction — no more than 2 impacts
- Warden Discipline — no more than 35 damage taken
- Speed Breach — complete the campaign within 180 seconds

## Replay flow

The title screen now contains a Challenge Matrix for doctrine and modifier selection. The mission report displays completed contracts, earned Core Fragments, mastery XP, level-ups, reward multiplier, best rank, and total progression.
