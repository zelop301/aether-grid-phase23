# Player Testing Checklist

Test with at least five people who have not seen the project before. Do not explain controls unless they are completely blocked.

## Record

- Time to first movement
- Time to first successful interaction
- Time to first successful attack
- Number of deaths
- Missed objectives
- Forgotten controls
- Motorcycle wall impacts
- Average and minimum FPS
- Mission completion time
- Point where the player quits or asks for help

## Observe

- Can the player find Relay Alpha without verbal help?
- Do enemy animations communicate blockable, parryable, and unblockable attacks?
- Does the player understand Health, Energy, Resolve, and the current objective?
- Does compact HUD remain readable?
- Can the player recover after failure using Restart Checkpoint?
- Does the Flux Cycle feel stable on keyboard and controller?
- Does autopilot communicate clearly when it is active?
- Are flashing, camera motion, text size, and audio comfortable?

## Acceptance target

At least four of five first-time players should complete Genesis Breach without developer intervention. Repeated confusion should be fixed before adding more content.
